### Panel Overview

This panel cuts through the hype to examine how organizations in legacy environments are deploying AI to drive real, measurable transformation—and what it takes to build, scale, and operate these systems responsibly.

**_Key topics covered_**

- How established enterprises are practically adopting AI beyond proof-of-concept
- Scaling AI solutions within legacy infrastructure and organizational constraints
- Reshaping enterprise roles and team structures around AI capabilities
- Responsible deployment: governance, risk, and operational considerations
- Lessons learned from real-world AI transformation efforts
